How the implied value is computed

Each category's ratio is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot's protocols in that category whose token already trades — the market's own answer to what a protocol of that kind is worth per dollar of TVL. A category with fewer than three traded tokens falls back to the global median. Growth is the protocol's own 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%. Maturity rewards a long record with no token: a protocol listed three years ago scores higher than one listed last month. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

Read it as a radar, not a promise: the snapshot does not say whether or when a token is issued, and farming costs real gas. Never pay anyone to claim for you — a real claim costs nothing but gas.
